Grain na zeleném pozadí

Same wash, same content, five treatments. The grain is generated the way the wash is — fractal noise in an SVG, a kilobyte, tiling seamlessly — not a photographed paper scan, so it costs nothing and never repeats visibly.

It multiplies over the wash, which means it only ever darkens and cannot shift the green. That matters here: --lime is the floor every contrast measurement on the site is taken against, so grain that darkened past it would quietly invalidate the harness. G1–G3 stay inside it; G4 and G5 go further and would need the floor re-derived.

Judge these at 100%, and on your phone. Grain is a per-pixel effect and a scaled screenshot is not evidence.

Jump to the sparser versions of G5 › — S1 and S2 keep the specks fine and make them rarer; S3 and S4 make them bigger and further apart. Those are the two things "řidší" can mean and they look nothing alike, so pick by pointing.

Jump to the wall grounds › — everything else on this page changes what sits OVER the wash. Those four change the wash itself, from cloud to wall, and each carries the S1 grain you picked.

Jump to the sharper W1 › — X4/X6/X10 print the same wall in four, six or ten flat inks instead of a continuous ramp, and Z0/Z1/Z2 do the same to the seam.

Jump to the dot matrix › — this is the one that matches the picture you sent. The gradient is carried by how many cells are lit, not by opacity, so there is no intermediate tone in it anywhere to go soft.
