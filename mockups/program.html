Program s kalendářem

Local only, real dates out of data/program.json — fourteen performances, September to December. The list stays under the calendar in both; tapping a day does not take it away.

Worth knowing before you pick: a month grid for three or four performances is mostly empty squares. P1 leans into that — the empty days are barely there, so the played ones are the only thing to look at. P2 drops the grid and shows the whole season at once, which for fourteen dates may simply be the more useful object.

P1 · Month grid

The calendar you asked for. Monday-first, Czech weekday heads. Only days with a performance are buttons; the rest are numbers. Tapping one marks it and jumps to that date in the list below — nothing is filtered away, so the list is still the whole programme.
