1 · Hero → page

All four are the same single element: the wash underneath, an ink layer on top, and a mask that decides where the ink stops. Only the mask changes, so they are directly comparable. A radial mask curves the seam; how far apart its stops sit is how much it blends.

4 · Repertoár as a deck

Drag the top card sideways and let go, or use the buttons, or the arrow keys. Past the threshold it is thrown in the direction you pushed it and goes to the back; short of it, it springs back. Four plays, so the deck is a loop rather than a pile that runs out.
